Popover

Popup-backed pieces keep separate `popover.*Style`, `menu.*Style`, and `listbox.*Style` contracts even when they share theme values.

Popover

Popover is the low-level anchored surface primitive, while related popup-backed controls still keep their own contracts.

Popover overview

Use the low-level popover primitive for anchored, dismissible panels like filters, inspectors, and view options.

Menu tokens

Menus own their own styling contract so themes can override menu structure without coupling it to listbox or popover consumers.
